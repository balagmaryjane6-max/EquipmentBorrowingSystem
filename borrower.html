Equipment Borrowing Policy & Guidelines

Institutional rules for responsible use of facilities and technical gear.

Loan Durations & Extensions

• Standard loan durations are 1 to 7 calendar days depending on equipment category.

• Special thesis or research projects may request up to 14 days with faculty endorsement.

• Extension requests must be submitted at least 24 hours prior to the return deadline.

• A maximum of 3 simultaneous active items is permitted per student borrower.

Care & Return Standards

• All accessories (power adapters, carry cases, lenses, cables) must be returned together.

• Equipment must be clean and batteries charged to at least 20% upon check-in.

• Custodians will perform a physical inspection and functional verification before signing off.

• Any damage or missing parts must be reported immediately upon discovery.
